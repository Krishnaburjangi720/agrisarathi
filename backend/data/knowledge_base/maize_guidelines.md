# Maize Farming Guidelines

**Source Reference:** Adapted from general FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization) and regional extension guidelines for maize cultivation.

## Soil Requirements
Maize grows well in deep, rich, well-drained loamy soils. The preferred pH is between 5.8 and 7.0. It requires high organic matter for optimal yield. Soil compaction should be avoided to allow deep root penetration.

## Irrigation
Maize is highly sensitive to water stress at the flowering (tasseling and silking) stage. It requires about 500-800 mm of water throughout the growing season. Ensure proper drainage to avoid water stagnation, as waterlogging for even 24 hours can significantly reduce yield.

## Risks
Major risks include drought stress during silking, which severely impacts grain setting, and pests such as the Fall Armyworm. Fungal diseases like stalk rot can also be a risk under overly moist conditions.
